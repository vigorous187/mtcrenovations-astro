## Transform Your Bathroom

A bathroom renovation in Burlington or Hamilton is usually a full room, not a new faucet. Homeowners come to us for a walk-in shower, a new vanity, tile that will hold up, and the plumbing and electrical work behind the walls. MTC Renovations coordinates that scope as one job for houses in Burlington, Hamilton, and [Oakville](/location/oakville/).

## What a bathroom renovation includes

The work we list for this service is the work we price:

- Walk-in shower conversions
- Full bathroom remodels
- Custom tile
- Vanity and countertop installation
- Plumbing upgrades
- Heated flooring
- Accessibility modifications
- Lighting and ventilation

A typical remodel runs from demolition through waterproofing, tile, the vanity, fixtures, and paint. Tub-to-shower conversions are the request we hear most often. The [Burlington bathroom renovation guide](/blog/bathroom-renovation-burlington/) puts a walk-in shower in an existing footprint at $6,000–$15,000, depending on size and tile. ## What bathroom renovations cost

The tiers below are the ones published in the Burlington bathroom guide. The same tiers apply to a bathroom remodel in Hamilton.

- **Basic update:** $15,000–$22,000. Fixtures and finishes are replaced without moving plumbing.
- **Mid-range full renovation:** $22,000–$38,000.
- **Ensuite or luxury renovation:** $38,000–$65,000+.

A basic update, in that guide, is a new vanity and sink, toilet, tile surround, flooring, lighting, exhaust fan, and paint, without moving walls or plumbing. The mid-range tier is a gut to the studs: walk-in shower, vanity, heated floors, and new fixtures. The ensuite tier adds the larger luxury scope, including a curbless shower. Those figures are ranges, not a quote. A fixed-price quote is written after we measure the room. The [renovation price guide](/estimate/) is a planning range before that visit. Photos of finished bathrooms are on the [bathroom project page](/bathroom/our-work/).

## Burlington and Hamilton

We renovate bathrooms in [Burlington](/location/burlington/) and [Hamilton](/location/hamilton/), including the Hamilton communities already on that city page: Ancaster, Dundas, Stoney Creek, and Waterdown. Oakville bathrooms are the same crew and the same written scope.

Older rooms often need more than new tile. A tub that is moving, a fan that was never ducted, or a wall that is soft behind the surround shows up once the room is opened. Newer rooms more often keep the layout and replace the vanity, tile, lighting, and shower glass.

## How long the room takes

Most standard bathroom renovations on this page are described as 2 to 3 weeks from demo to final install. The site-wide FAQ gives a bathroom remodel as 2–4 weeks, depending on scope, permit processing, material lead times, and scheduling. The schedule in the quote is the one for your room. We protect the rest of the house, clean up daily, and walk the finished room with you before we call it done.

The sequence on our bathroom jobs is a free consultation, a design and fixed-price quote, material selection for tile, the vanity, and fixtures, construction, and a final walkthrough.
