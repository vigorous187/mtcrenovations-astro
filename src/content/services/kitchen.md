## Your Dream Kitchen Awaits

A kitchen renovation in Hamilton or Burlington is usually the room that sets the rest of the project. The kitchen is where the household actually lives, so the work has to function on a Tuesday morning, not only look right in a photo. MTC Renovations builds complete kitchen transformations for homeowners in Hamilton, Burlington, and Oakville: cabinets, countertops, lighting, and the trades behind the walls.

## What a kitchen renovation includes

The scope on a typical kitchen comes from the work we already list for this service:

- Custom cabinet installation
- Quartz and granite countertops
- Open-concept layout conversions
- Backsplash tile
- Plumbing and electrical upgrades
- Island builds
- Flooring replacement
- Lighting design

From cabinet selection to countertop installation, backsplash tile to under-cabinet lighting, we handle the room as one job. That includes demolition, plumbing, electrical, framing, drywall, cabinetry, countertops, flooring, paint, and trim. You get one crew and a written scope instead of hiring each trade yourself.

Layout is the decision that changes everything else. Keeping the sink, stove, and fridge where they are avoids moving plumbing and electrical. Opening a wall into the dining room, adding an island, or relocating the sink is a different project, and it belongs in the quote before demo starts. Material choices — cabinet line, countertop, appliances, and flooring — are the other half of the price. We walk through those selections with you and write them into the scope.

## How kitchen pricing works

Hamilton and Burlington kitchen renovations run from $25,000 to $75,000+. Final pricing depends on size, materials and site conditions. We provide transparent quotes so there are never surprises.

- **Refresh** — same layout, with cabinet refacing or paint, counters, backsplash and fixtures: about $25,000–$40,000
- **Mid-range** — new cabinets and counters, flooring, lighting, and some plumbing or electrical moves: about $40,000–$60,000
- **Full remodel** — layout change, walls moved, custom cabinets and premium finishes: $60,000–$75,000+

That range is the figure published on this page. It is not a quote. A fixed-price quote comes after we see the kitchen, measure it, and write down what is included. The number on the quote is the number for that scope. If you change the scope, the quote changes with it.
